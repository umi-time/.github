# Code of Conduct  
Umi‑Time Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct  
(Adapted for the Umi‑Time project)

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the Umi‑Time project a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints  
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully  
- Focusing on what is best for the project  
- Showing appreciation for contributions  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination  
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks  
- Publishing private information without permission  
- Disruptive behavior that interferes with project collaboration  

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories  
- Issues and pull requests  
- Discussions  
- Project documentation  
- Community spaces related to Umi‑Time  

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

**security@umitime.com**  

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will follow these steps in response to violations:

1. **Correction** — A private, gentle reminder of expected behavior  
2. **Warning** — A clear warning that repeated behavior will result in consequences  
3. **Temporary Ban** — Temporary removal from project spaces  
4. **Permanent Ban** — Permanent removal for severe or repeated violations
